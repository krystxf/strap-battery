Privacy Policy

Strap Battery · Last updated November 14, 2026

Strap Battery does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives any information about you or your sensors.

What the app stores

To show battery levels and heart rate, the app stores the following on your devices only:

  • The names, battery levels, battery history and device information (model, firmware, serial number) of the heart rate sensors you use.
  • Heart rate readings from the current session (up to 6 hours), so the chart and export keep working after you reopen the app.
  • Your settings, such as charging notifications.

Where it goes

  • Your Apple Watch: sensor and battery information is synced between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch through Apple’s Watch Connectivity.
  • iCloud: if you use iCloud, sensors, battery history and settings are saved in your private iCloud storage so your iPhone, iPad and Mac show the same data. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is not accessible to the developer.
  • Exports: heart rate is only shared when you export a CSV file yourself.
  • Apple Health: if you turn on “Save Heart Rate to Health” in Settings, each heart rate reading is saved to Apple Health on your iPhone, with the name, manufacturer, model and hardware and firmware versions of the sensor that measured it. The app only writes heart rate; it never reads any Health data. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties and never leaves your device through the app. You can stop it with the same switch, and remove the saved readings in the Health app.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to heart rate sensors and read their heart rate, battery and device information.

Deleting your data

Forget a sensor in the app, clear heart rate data from the Heart Rate tab, or delete the app. Heart rate saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. To remove iCloud data, turn off iCloud for Strap Battery in Settings or delete the app’s data under iCloud › Manage Storage.
