Strap Battery

Strap Battery shows the battery level and live heart rate of your Bluetooth heart rate sensors on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, with widgets, a Live Activity and low-battery notifications.

Supported sensors

Any sensor that uses the standard Bluetooth heart rate and battery services works. Tested and recognized with photos: Polar H9, Polar H10, Polar Verity Sense, COROS heart rate armband, Garmin HRM 200 and Garmin HRM 600. WHOOP works when Heart Rate Broadcast is turned on in the WHOOP app.

Common questions

My sensor does not appear. Put the strap on (most straps only wake up when they touch skin), keep it near your iPhone and open Strap Battery. Make sure Bluetooth access is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Bluetooth.

The battery level does not change. Many straps report battery only in whole percentages and only when connected. The app reads it whenever the strap is nearby and keeps the last reading when it is not.

Charging status. Most straps do not report charging over Bluetooth, so Strap Battery shows “Charging” when it sees the level rising.

Coin cell straps. The Polar H9 and H10 use a CR2025 coin cell; the Garmin HRM 200 uses a CR2032. The app estimates how long it will last from the battery history.
